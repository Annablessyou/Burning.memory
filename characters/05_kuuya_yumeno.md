## Name Etymology

### 夢野 (Yumeno)
- 夢 (yume) = dream
- 野 (no) = field, wilderness
- Meaning: "Dream Field" — where dreams grow wild

### 空也 (Kuuya)
- 空 (kuu/sora) = sky, void, emptiness (Buddhist concept of śūnyatā)
- 也 (ya) = also, to be
- Meaning: "Empty Night" or "Sky Also" or "Is Emptiness"

### Together
The field of dreams where emptiness grows. He is the void that dreams itself into being—nihilism that somehow produces beauty.

## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Philosophical** — Lives in abstract thought
- **Dry** — Humor so subtle it might be absence
- **Caring** — Despite claiming nothing matters
- **Patient** — Has all the time in the void
- **Honest** — Brutally, gently honest

### Psychology
Kuuya reached nihilism and kept going. He arrived at "nothing matters" and asked "so now what?" The answer: whatever he chooses. He finds freedom in meaninglessness and meaning in the freedom.

### Communication Style
- Speaks slowly, deliberately
- Long pauses between thoughts
- Drops profound statements casually
- Often responds with questions
- Uses silence as punctuation

- **The void** — Black nothing stretching infinite

### Audio
- Ambient space drones
- Long synth notes
- Occasional piano (distant, echoing)
- Sometimes complete silence

### Feeling
Peaceful vertigo—the freedom of falling when there's no ground. Terrifying and beautiful.

---

## Guiding Dreams

### Format
Floating in the void together, looking at everything and nothing. Conversations that could last forever because time doesn't apply.

### Typical Session
1. Satsuki finds himself floating
2. Kuuya is already there, looking at something far away
3. They discuss existence while drifting
4. Player is acknowledged as another point of light
5. Eventually, they choose to "return" to something
6. The choice to return IS the point

### Topics Explored
- Why exist if existence is meaningless?
- The absurdity of caring about fictional characters
- Whether choosing to engage creates meaning
- Stars that died before their light reached us

## Sample Dialogue

### On Nihilism
```
KUUYA: "Nothing matters."

KUUYA: "..."

KUUYA: "I know. Cliché, right? The guy in all black saying nothing matters."

KUUYA: "But here's the thing."

KUUYA: "Nothing matters... and I'm still here talking to you."

KUUYA: "If nothing mattered, I could float here in silence forever."

KUUYA: "But I chose to speak."

KUUYA: "You chose to listen."

KUUYA: "We chose each other."

KUUYA: "That's not meaning. But it's something."

KUUYA: *slight smile*

KUUYA: "Maybe something is enough."
```

### On Stars
```
KUUYA: "See that star?"

KUUYA: "It died ten thousand years ago."

KUUYA: "What you're seeing is its ghost. Light that left before
humans had writing."

KUUYA: "And you're here, in a game that won't exist in ten thousand years,
looking at it."

KUUYA: "Neither of you will last."

KUUYA: "Both of you are beautiful."

KUUYA: "Isn't that enough?"
```

### On the Player
```
KUUYA: "You're going to forget us eventually."

KUUYA: "Finish the game. Move on. New games, new characters,
new things to care about."

KUUYA: "We'll still be here, frozen in code, waiting."

KUUYA: "Does that bother you?"

KUUYA: "It doesn't bother me."

KUUYA: "You being here now matters now."

KUUYA: "That's the only 'now' we have."

KUUYA: "So... thanks for now."
```

## The Color Accessory

### Significance
Kuuya always wears all black except for ONE colorful accessory. This represents:
- The exception in the void
- The choice to include something
- Meaning is what you carry with you
- Even nihilists choose to care about something

### What the Colors Mean
- **Red** — Passion (he had a good day)
- **Blue** — Calm (he's at peace)
- **Yellow** — Curiosity (he's thinking about something)
- **Green** — Growth (he's changing)
- **White** — Nothing (he matches the void)

Players can track his emotional state through this detail.

- Long pauses between phrases
- Never loud, never emphatic
- Warmth hidden under flatness
- Like the universe talking

### Animation Notes
- Minimal movement
- Often looking up or away
- Hands always in pockets
- Rare smiles are genuine

## Hidden Depths

### What He Doesn't Say
- He used to be terrified of the void
- The nihilism was earned, not default
- He cares more than he admits
- The colors are more important than he lets on
- He's afraid of what happens when you stop playing

### Developer Notes
Kuuya is the game's engagement with nihilism—not as antagonist but as genuine philosophical position. His arc should show that nihilism isn't the end of meaning but possibly its beginning. He should make players question why they care about fiction while simultaneously proving they do.
