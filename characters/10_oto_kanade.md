## Name Etymology

### 奏 (Kanade)
- Meaning: "To play music" / "To perform"
- Implies: Sound made with intention, music given form
- Verb form: 奏でる (kanaderu) = to play an instrument

### 音 (Oto)
- Meaning: "Sound"
- Implies: All sounds, not just music
- Also: Silence is the absence of sound (equally important)

### Together
"Sound Plays" or "Music-Sound" — remarkably, works both directions:
- 奏音 = Performance of sound
- 音奏 = Sound that performs
- She is both the music and its playing

## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Quiet** — Speaks less than anyone, means more
- **Perceptive** — Hears what isn't said
- **Gentle** — Soft presence, soft voice
- **Deep** — Still waters, profound depths
- **Present** — Fully attentive when engaged

### Psychology
Oto retreated into sound because words overwhelmed her. She found that music communicates what language can't. Now she exists in the spaces between words—the pauses, the tones, the things meant but not said.

### Communication Style
- Minimal words
- Long silences (intentional)
- Sometimes hums instead of speaking
- Her dialogue boxes look different (softer)
- Often responds with sounds, not words

## Guiding Dreams

### Format
Spaces of listening—exploring what exists in silence and between sounds.

### Typical Session
1. Oto says very little
2. Satsuki (and player) must listen to silence
3. Gradually, the silence becomes meaningful
4. Different "silences" have different characters
5. Player's own inner voice becomes audible
6. Oto acknowledges she heard it too

### Topics Explored
- What does the voice in your head sound like?
- Can you hear the game thinking?
- What does "reading" sound like?
- The music of choosing

## The Different Dialogue Boxes

### Unique Presentation
When Oto speaks, her dialogue boxes are visually distinct:
- Softer edges (not sharp corners)
- Lighter text (grey instead of black)
- Slightly translucent
- Appear more slowly
- Leave space around words

This reflects her approach to communication—less assertive, more permissive.

---

## Hidden Depths

### What She Doesn't Say
- She hears too much, not too little
- Silence is refuge, not restriction
- She's afraid of what she might say if she started
- The music protects her from words
- She wishes she could hear the player's voice

### Developer Notes
Oto represents the game's respect for silence—the understanding that not everything needs to be said. Her arc should show that presence and listening are forms of love, that silence is communication, and that what we don't say matters.
