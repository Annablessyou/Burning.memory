## Name Etymology

### 理 (Kotowari/Ri)
- Meaning: "Reason" / "Logic" / "Natural order" / "Truth"
- In philosophy: 理 represents the underlying principle of all things
- Reading: Usually "Ri" but can be read "Kotowari" (to explain, to reason)

### 鈴 (Rei)
- Meaning: "Bell"
- Also: Can be read as "Rei" which sounds like 零 (zero)
- Double meaning: "Bell of Reason" AND "Logic Zero" (the paradox of pure logic being somehow illogical)

### Together
The bell that rings clear with truth—or the zero at the heart of all mathematics. She IS the paradox she studies.
