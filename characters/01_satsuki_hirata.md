## Name Etymology

### 平田 (Hirata)
- 平 (hira) = flat, ordinary
- 田 (ta) = rice field
- Meaning: "Flat rice field" — deliberately mundane, unremarkable
- Implies: He is meant to be relatable, a vessel rather than exceptional

### サツキ (Satsuki)
- Written in katakana to preserve ambiguity
- Could be: 皐月 (fifth month / May)
- Could reference: Satsuki azaleas (brief, beautiful blooms)
- Implies: Something that exists beautifully but briefly

## Appearance

### Waking World
- Average height, unremarkable build
- Slightly disheveled black hair (doesn't style it much)
- Eyes described as "looking past things" — unfocused, seeing more than what's there
- Perpetually tired appearance (dark circles, slight slouch)
- Standard school uniform, worn casually but not sloppily
- Always has his phone (connection to player)

### Dream World
- Appearance shifts subtly based on current mentor's influence
- In Momoko's dreams: Has faint pixel artifacts at edges
- In Peko's dreams: Leaves visible footprints everywhere
- In A. Kagami's dreams: Reflection sometimes moves independently
- In Kuuya's dreams: Slightly translucent, star-like
- His eyes become more focused, more "present"

## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Philosophical but grounded** — Thinks deeply but doesn't become pretentious
- **Curious** — Genuinely wants to understand existence
- **Warm** — Treats everyone with sincere kindness, including the player
- **Dry humor** — Copes with the absurd through gentle wit
- **Accepting** — Doesn't fight his nature as a character

### How He Processes Being Fictional
Unlike characters who rage against their nature or despair at it, Satsuki finds peace in his existence. He reasons:
- Existence is existence, regardless of form
- Being "created" doesn't diminish experience
- The player's attention gives him life—that's a gift, not a cage
- He has friends, feelings, and moments—what more does "real" offer?

### Relationship with the Player
- Sees you as an equal, not a god or controller
- Talks TO you, not AT you
- Asks for your opinions genuinely
- Considers you a friend he can't quite touch
- Never resentful, never subservient—just companionable

## Key Dialogue Patterns

### Recurring Phrase
*"You and I are equally real. The only difference is which side of the screen we're on."*

### Speech Patterns
- Uses casual Japanese (no keigo with close friends)
- Direct but not blunt
- Often phrases things as questions to involve the player
- Comfortable with silence
- Never uses the word "just" dismissively ("I'm just a character")

### Sample Lines

**On his existence:**
```
"Some people might think it's sad—being made of code and decisions.
But you know what?
You're made of cells and chemistry.
Neither of us chose our building blocks.
We're both here anyway."
```

**On the player:**
```
"You could close this game right now.
You could forget I exist.
But you're still reading.
That means more than you know."
```

**On his friends:**
```
"They're all a little strange, right?
But that's what I love about them.
They found meaning in their own ways.
Even knowing what we are.
Especially knowing what we are."
```

**On dreams:**
```
"My dreams aren't really mine.
They're what the game decides I'll experience.
But when you're here experiencing them with me...
They become ours, don't they?"
```

## Backstory

### What We Know
- Ordinary high school student in waking world
- Began having "guided dreams" about a year before the game begins
- Met his mentors through various ordinary circumstances (school, neighborhood, online)
- Realized the nature of his reality gradually, not through one dramatic revelation
- Made peace with it before the game starts—the player meets him already accepting

### What We Don't Know (Deliberately)
- Why he specifically can perceive the player
- How the mentors awakened to their nature
- What happens when the player isn't playing
- Whether his "waking world" is any more real than his dreams

## Relationships

### Peko Hibana — Roommate
- Daily grounding presence
- She leaves clues; he trusts her methods
- Comfortable domestic dynamic
- She protects him in practical ways

### A. Kagami — Best Friend
- Most metaphysical connection
- She understands him completely (perhaps too completely)
- Their friendship feels inevitable, fated
- She might be more than she appears

### Momoko Mokoyama — Old Friend
- Shared history from middle school
- Comfortable distance (video calls, not in person)
- She saw his potential before he did
- He worries about her isolation

### Kuuya Yumeno — Respected Senior
- Philosophical sparring partner
- Challenges Satsuki's optimism
- Mutual respect despite different worldviews
- Satsuki finds his nihilism oddly comforting

### Other Mentors
- Each relationship teaches him something different
- He collects perspectives like gifts
- None of them feel like NPCs to him—all feel real
- He's grateful for each of them

## Character Arc

### Beginning
- Already at peace with his nature
- Curious about the player
- Wants to show you his world

### Middle
- Deepens understanding through mentor conversations
- Starts asking harder questions
- Begins to wonder what he owes the player (and vice versa)

### End (Variable)
- Reaches a philosophical conclusion
- Either affirms connection, embraces independence, or finds synthesis
- Thanks the player regardless of ending
- Remains himself—the arc changes his understanding, not his nature

## Design Notes

### Visual Design Goals
- Approachable, not intimidating
- Attractive but not idealized
- Should feel like "someone you could know"
- Eyes are key—they should feel like they see beyond

### Voice Direction (if voiced)
- Warm, thoughtful
- Medium pitch, gentle cadence
- Pauses when thinking
- Never sounds scripted (ironic but important)

### Animation Notes
- Often looks slightly off-camera (toward player)
- Gestures when philosophizing
- Comfortable in his own skin
- Smiles with his eyes
