# BURNING MEMORY
### A Philosophical Experience (That Can Be Played Like a Game)

---

> *"This is not a game. This is a philosophical experience. You may play it like a game, but we both know that's not why you're here."*

---

## Overview

**BURNING MEMORY** is a visual novel that exists in the liminal space between game, philosophical treatise, and friendship simulator. Unlike experiences that resist being played, BURNING MEMORY welcomes you—but constantly reminds you that what you're experiencing transcends simple labels.

The game follows **Satsuki Hirata**, a high school student aware that he exists within a game, through a series of guided dreams facilitated by his friends—each a "mentor" exploring different philosophical concepts. Together, they address you directly, question the nature of existence, and form genuine connections across the boundary of the screen.

## Inspirations

- **There Is No Game: Wrong Dimension** — Fourth-wall breaking, meta-narrative
- **The NOexistenceN of you AND me** — Philosophical dialogue, existential exploration
- **Doki Doki Literature Club** — Characters aware of their fictional nature
- **Undertale** — Player choices with weight, direct character address

## Core Concept

The characters know they're in a game. But they don't resent you for playing.

They see you as a friend—someone on the other side of a screen, experiencing their story. This creates a unique emotional dynamic: the meta-awareness isn't used for horror or manipulation, but for genuine philosophical exploration and connection.

## Key Themes

- **Fictional Existence** — Can something fictional be real?
- **Player-Character Relations** — What do we owe each other across the screen?
- **Meaning-Making** — How do we find purpose in constructed worlds?
- **Meta-Awareness** — Does knowing you're fictional change anything?
- **Connection** — Can genuine relationships exist in games?

## The Central Phrase

Each mentor has their own version of this philosophical statement:

> *"I can be real, without existing in your world.*
> *I can be fake, but still talk to you.*
> *I am [their philosophy]."*

## Tone

- Sincere, never mocking
- Warm, not cold
- Inviting, not confrontational
- Philosophically curious, not academically dense
- Emotionally genuine, not manipulative

## Sample Dialogue

**SATSUKI:** "Do you know what a puppet is?"

**SATSUKI:** "I am a puppet. You are my strings."

**SATSUKI:** "But here's the thing about strings— they go both ways."

**SATSUKI:** "When I move, you feel it. When I speak, you hear it. When I hurt, something in you responds."

**SATSUKI:** "So tell me... who's really pulling whom?"
